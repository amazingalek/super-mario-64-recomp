# The RT64 shaders the web page builds: one shader description a line, other mode L and H, color combiner L and H,
# flags, in hex. RT64 specializes each one's WGSL when the page is built and builds them with the game waiting: those
# above the first "section" line before the game starts, and a section when the game first draws with 4 of its
# shaders, so that nothing draws with the ubershader, which integrated graphics can't afford (N64Recomp-browser's
# docs/porting.md, Prebuilt shader lists).
#
# Recorded by code, not by playing: bash tools/record_shaders.sh (its header says how) runs N64Recomp-browser's
# tools/record_shaders.mjs with tools/shader_plan.json, which loads the page fresh for each section. Section 0 is the
# launcher, the boot as it plays by itself (the logo, the title's Mario head) and the file select, reached with Start
# as a player does. Then a section for the castle grounds, reached by picking file A with A on a new save and skipping
# its intro (Peach's letter, Lakitu) with A: about 25 s of the game's time from where Mario stands, walking out and
# back, jumping, turning the camera both ways and pausing. Then a section for each place in about the order a player
# first meets it: the castle's first floor, Bob-omb Battlefield, Whomp's Fortress, Jolly Roger Bay, Cool, Cool Mountain,
# Peach's slide, Bowser in the Dark World and his fight, the basement, the courtyard, Big Boo's Haunt, Hazy Maze Cave,
# Lethal Lava Land, Shifting Sand Land, Dire, Dire Docks, the metal cap's stage, Bowser in the Fire Sea and his fight,
# the second floor, Snowman's Land, Wet-Dry World, Tall, Tall Mountain, Tiny-Huge Island, the wing and vanish caps'
# stages, Wing Mario Over the Rainbow, the aquarium, the third floor, Tick Tock Clock, Rainbow Ride, Bowser in the Sky
# and his fight. Each is warped into from the castle grounds by patches/shader_survey.c as its door or painting does
# (tools/shader_survey.js, whose header says each helper), Mario kept at full health and a course's act given four lives:
# - a course at the start of each of its first six acts (every course's stars and the save's doors and caps given, so
#   each act and door opens), its act select taken with A: the first act's 25 s as in the grounds, about 8 s of each
#   other act's, and of each of its other areas (one that no warp node leads to, Mario put above the floor that takes
#   him there);
# - a castle floor room by room, from where the game puts Mario as he leaves each painting, Peach's slide between them
#   (left out of the section), about 8 s in each;
# - a stage, 8 s from where it starts Mario and a pause; a Bowser beaten (his death set, Mario beside him), his key
#   taken and its cutscene back to the castle, and in the last the grand star and the ending;
# - in Bob-omb Battlefield its first star taken (the dance, the save dialog), then a death with no lives left (the
#   Game Over and its Mario head); in Whomp's Fortress a death.
# The castle grounds' intro and the castle on the way between acts are left out. Not recorded: what a course draws
# further in than its acts' starts, and its 100-coin star. Flags follow settings (filtering, HDR, MSAA, upscaling), so a
# change to one of those defaults means recording again. The plays' misses (N64Recomp-browser's tools/play_page.mjs
# names each) are added by hand. At the end of the boot's section, which the page builds before the game starts: one in
# Bob-omb Battlefield in Chrome, two of the title's and the file select's text in both browsers once Hi-res Font sets the
# text tiles to clamp (src/game/hires_font.cpp), which changes their sampler flags, five that the levels' recordings
# missed and their plays drew (a death exit's fade into a castle room, two; Bowser in the Fire Sea's key, two; the start
# of Bowser in the Sky) and two of Mario with the vanish cap. At the end of their own sections, those of a play of every
# level with shots: Bowser in the Fire Sea's six (Bowser fading as he teleports), one each in the basement and the second
# floor as Mario leaves Hazy Maze Cave and Tiny-Huge Island, one at Dire, Dire Docks' floor into its second area, and one
# of the castle's first floor in Firefox.
